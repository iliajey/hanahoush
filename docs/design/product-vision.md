# Hanahoush — Product Vision

> The vision, mission, brand, audience and strategy that guide every design
> decision for the Hanahoush public website and platform.

---

## Mission

Empower enterprises in the region to build, modernize and scale their software
with engineering that feels effortless: reliable ERP, intelligent automation,
and web platforms that look and behave like the best products in the world.

## Vision

To be the trusted engineering partner that companies think of first when they
want enterprise software that is both technically excellent and beautifully
designed — a local brand with global product standards, comparable to Stripe,
Linear, Framer, Raycast, Vercel and Clerk in perceived quality.

## Brand Personality

- **Expert but approachable** — we speak engineering precisely, without jargon walls.
- **Premium and calm** — confident, unhurried interfaces; no noise.
- **Modern and precise** — crisp typography, deliberate motion, consistent systems.
- **Bilingual-native** — Persian-first, fully fluent in English and Arabic, with RTL/LTR as a first-class concern, never an afterthought.

## Target Audience

Primary:
- **Enterprise decision makers** — CTOs, IT directors, operations heads who need
  ERP (hanRP), Odoo implementation, and reliable enterprise development.
- **Product owners / technical managers** — evaluating outsourcing and
  co-development partners for web applications and programming services.

Secondary:
- **Founders & SMEs** — organizations growing beyond spreadsheets that need a
  serious technology partner.
- **Technical talent & recruits** — developers assessing Hanahoush as an employer.

## Business Goals

1. Generate qualified project inquiries (lead capture) from enterprise buyers.
2. Position Hanahoush as a premium, multi-capability engineering firm.
3. Communicate six service pillars: Enterprise Software, ERP (hanRP), Odoo,
   AI, Web Applications, Programming Services.
4. Demonstrate credibility through projects, articles, technologies, and a
   company timeline.
5. Convert visitors through a clear, low-friction contact path and a CTA that
   matches intent (estimate, book a call, request a proposal).

## Competitive Advantage

- **Deep ERP + AI + web depth in one house** — not a generalist agency.
- **hanRP**, Hanahoush's own ERP product line, gives the site a differentiated
  product story rather than a generic service list.
- **Design quality as a differentiator** — the website itself is the first
  proof of craft.
- **Multilingual, RTL-first product thinking** in a region where most enterprise
  sites are translated afterthoughts.

## User Personas

| Persona | Profile | Primary need | Primary CTA |
|---------|---------|--------------|-------------|
| **Ali — Enterprise CTO** | 40+, oversees IT at a 500-person company | Replace aging ERP / legacy systems with low risk | "Request a proposal" / "Talk to engineering" |
| **Sara — Operations Director** | 35+, runs procurement & supply chain | Odoo or hanRP that fits real workflows | "Book a consultation" |
| **Reza — Product Owner** | 30+, building a web app with a small team | Trusted co-development partner | "Estimate a project" |
| **Mina — Founder/SME** | 28+, growing startup | First serious tech partner | "Start a project" |

## Customer Journey

1. **Discover** — lands via search or referral; hero states value instantly.
2. **Evaluate** — browses services, hanRP, projects, technologies, articles.
3. **Validate** — reads testimonials, partners, timeline, case studies.
4. **Act** — books a call / requests an estimate via the contact flow.
5. **Onboard** — receives a response, proposal, and discovery meeting.
6. **Deliver & advocate** — becomes a reference and returns for more.

## Pain Points We Address

- Vendors that promise "enterprise" but ship amateur results.
- Generic agency sites that cannot prove engineering depth.
- RTL/Arabic/Persian afterthoughts and broken bidirectional typography.
- Opaque pricing, unclear process, and slow communication.
- Fear of lock-in and legacy debt during ERP migration.

## Business Values

- **Craft over volume** — fewer, better projects.
- **Transparency** — clear process, clear pricing models, clear communication.
- **Engineering integrity** — clean architecture, security, maintainability.
- **Client partnership** — we win when the client's product wins.
- **Regional excellence** — Persian and Arabic first-class, global standards.
